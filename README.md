# Downshaft

A 3D descent game for [start.ng](https://start.ng), inspired by the classic NS-Shaft.
The ledges keep rising toward a ceiling of spikes, so keep dropping down without falling into the dark.

- **Stone** +1 HP · **Spikes** −4 HP · **Conveyor** carries you · **Spring** bounces you · **Cracked** gives way
- The ceiling costs 4 HP and knocks you through the ledge you're on

Controls: ← → or A D to move, P to pause, M for sound. On touch screens, hold the left or right side.

Plays as your start.ng character and saves your deepest floor to the start.ng leaderboard.
Static files only (three.js from jsDelivr), so there's no build step: open `index.html` through any static server.

The character model is adapted from start.ng's [Hangout](https://github.com/Blackburn0/startng-hangout) example.
